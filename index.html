Cyber Scam Awareness

1. Phishing Scams

Fraudsters send fake emails, messages or websites to steal personal information.

Safety tip: Verify the sender and website before sharing information.

2. UPI and OTP Fraud

Scammers may trick people into revealing OTPs or approving unexpected payment requests.

Safety tip: Never share your OTP or UPI PIN. You do not need a UPI PIN to receive money.

3. Fake Job Offers

Fraudsters may promise jobs and demand money for registration, training or guaranteed selection.

Safety tip: Verify the employer using official company contact details.

4. Digital Arrest Scams

Scammers impersonate officials and use threats or video calls to pressure people into transferring money.

Safety tip: Do not transfer money because of threats. Independently contact the relevant agency.

5. Malicious Applications

Some apps misuse permissions or attempt to collect personal information.

Safety tip: Install apps from trusted sources and review their permissions.

6. Fake Prize Messages

Unexpected messages may claim you have won a prize and ask for a fee or personal details.

Safety tip: Do not pay unexpected fees or share sensitive information to claim a prize.

Report Cybercrime in India

What should you do?

  1. Contact your bank or payment provider immediately if money has been lost.
  2. For financial cyber fraud in India, call 1930 as soon as possible.
  3. Submit a report through the official cybercrime portal.
  4. Keep relevant messages, transaction details and other evidence.
  5. Do not share passwords, OTPs or UPI PINs with anyone.

Official reporting website: National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

Remember

Act quickly, contact the appropriate authorities and use official websites for reporting.
